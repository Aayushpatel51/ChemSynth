# ChemSynth: Synthesis Edition (Intermediates Manufacturing)

This edition of ChemSynth focuses on the production of specialty chemical intermediates—the surfactants, esters, and emulsifiers that are themselves used to create consumer goods. Examples include Sorbitan Mono Laurate (Span 20), Glycerol Monostearate (GMS), and other non-ionic surfactants.

## 1. The Expert Knowledge Base: Synthesis Protocols
Instead of just mixing ingredients, this system manages Chemical Reactions.

### A. Raw Material Precursors
* **Polyols:** Sorbitol (70% solution or anhydrous), Glycerin, Ethylene Glycol.
* **Fatty Acids:** Lauric Acid, Stearic Acid, Oleic Acid (Vegetable or Tallow based).
* **Catalyst Systems:** Phosphoric Acid, Sodium Hydroxide, Sulphuric Acid, or specialized ion-exchange resins.
* **Auxiliaries:** Bleaching agents (Hydrogen Peroxide), Activated Carbon (for color removal).

### B. Reaction Logic Engine
* **Stoichiometric Calculator:** Calculates exact molar ratios required to achieve the desired degree of esterification.
* **Hydroxyl Value Prediction:** Predicts final chemical markers (Hydroxyl value, Saponification value, Acid value) based on raw material purity.
* **Equilibrium Monitoring:** Estimates water removal requirements to drive the esterification reaction to completion.

## 2. The Digital Manufacturing Process (Example: Sorbitan Mono Laurate)
### Step 1: The Input Requirements
* **Target Grade:** Cosmetic Grade (Low color, low odor).
* **Batch Size:** 5,000 kg.

### Step 2: The Formula (Bill of Materials)
* Sorbitol (70%): 2,150 kg
* Lauric Acid (99%): 2,380 kg
* Catalyst (H3PO4): 12 kg
* Bleaching agent (H2O2): 5 kg

### Step 3: The Detailed Process Protocol
* **Dehydration:** Charge Sorbitol to the reactor. Heat to 110°C under vacuum (600 mmHg) to remove water until <1% moisture.
* **Esterification:** Add Lauric Acid and Catalyst. Raise temperature to 200°C–210°C under a Nitrogen blanket.
* **Reaction Monitoring:** Acid Value testing instructions. Predicted completion when Acid Value < 5 mg KOH/g.
* **Refining:** Cool to 80°C. Add Activated Carbon for de-colorization. Stir for 60 minutes.
* **Filtration:** Pump through a Filter Press to remove residues.

## 3. Key User Features
* **Yield Calculator:** Predicts "Spent Water" vs. final product yield.
* **Grade Optimization:** Adjusts reaction parameters for different grades (e.g., Technical vs. Food Grade).
* **Equipment Integration:** Customizable to specific reactor volume and heating capabilities.

## 4. Why This Solves the "Engineer Gap"
Small and medium chemical plants often lack internal R&D for defining exact synthesis parameters. ChemSynth Synthesis Edition provides the Recipe and SOP that would normally require a senior Process Engineer.
